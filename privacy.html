Privacy

Short version: Nozomi only stores what it needs to save your study progress.

Without an account

Your progress (which cards you've studied and mastered, review dates, points, streak, stamps and settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.

With an account

  • What's stored: your email address (or your Google account's email if you sign in with Google), and one copy of the progress record described above.
  • Where: in a database hosted by Supabase. Database rules make sure each account can only read and change its own progress.
  • Why: only so your progress follows you between devices. It isn't sold, shared or used for advertising, and Nozomi has no ads or tracking.

Deleting your data

On the account page you can delete the progress saved to your account at any time. To delete your whole account (including your email), contact the site owner and it will be removed.

Voice

Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in speech voices. Depending on your browser, the text being read may be processed by your browser's own speech service.
